Cadence Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Cadence is a care-coordination application for private groups of caregivers. This policy describes how information is handled when you use Cadence.

Information handled by Cadence

Cadence may store information entered by users, including caregiver names, care schedules, completed-care history, medication schedules, medication administration history, notes, coverage information, and device synchronization status.

Storage and synchronization

Cadence stores information locally on your Apple devices and synchronizes shared household information through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services. The developer does not operate a separate server containing your Cadence household data.

Sharing

Household information is shared only with people invited to the household through Apple's iCloud sharing system. A household owner can manage or revoke participants. Users should invite only people authorized to view the household's care information.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Cadence does not contain third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics services. Apple may process diagnostic, crash, and TestFlight information according to Apple's privacy policies and the device owner's settings.

Notifications

Cadence uses local notifications, alarms, and CloudKit change notifications to provide reminders and keep participating devices synchronized. Notification contents are designed to limit sensitive details on the lock screen.

Data control and deletion

Users can remove local application data by deleting the app. Household owners can remove participants through iCloud sharing. To request help with access or deletion, contact the developer using the address below. Information stored by Apple is also subject to the user's iCloud settings and Apple's applicable policies.

Children and medical information

Cadence may be used by caregivers to coordinate care for another person. It is a scheduling and record-keeping aid and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice. Users are responsible for entering accurate information and following guidance from qualified healthcare professionals.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Cadence changes. The latest version will remain available at this URL, with its effective date shown above.
