Cadence Support

Cadence helps private care teams coordinate care schedules, caregiver coverage, medication administration, and time-sensitive reminders.

Contact support

For help, bug reports, or privacy questions, email lexical.older_4n@icloud.com.

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your Cadence version and build number
  • Your iPhone and Apple Watch models, if applicable
  • Your iOS and watchOS versions
  • A description of what happened and what you expected

Please do not include names, medication details, medical records, or other sensitive health information unless it is necessary to investigate the problem.

Synchronization problems

  • Confirm that the device is signed in to an Apple Account with iCloud enabled.
  • Confirm that Cadence is allowed to use iCloud.
  • Open Cadence on each device and allow a few moments for synchronization.
  • Check People → Care-team health for device and synchronization status.
  • Confirm that invited caregivers accepted the iCloud sharing invitation.

Notifications and alarms

  • Open iOS Settings and confirm that notifications are enabled for Cadence.
  • Allow sounds, time-sensitive notifications, and ringing alarms when prompted.
  • Cadence shows the current permission and coverage status within the app.

Apple Watch

  • Confirm that Cadence is installed on the paired Apple Watch.
  • Open Cadence once on both the iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Confirm that both devices use the same Apple Account and household.
